Our Daily Breads, Pastries & Cakes

Everything in our cases is baked fresh each morning using organic stone-milled flours and natural wild fermentation. Because we bake in small batches, items are available daily until our shelves are empty. We do not offer online checkout, but we warmly welcome pre-orders for guaranteed pickup!

Artisan Sourdough & Daily Breads

Our sourdough loaves take three days from feeding the wild starter to the final bake in our deck oven, creating a crunchy blistered crust and open crumb.
