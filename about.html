About North Star Bakery

Founded in 2018 in the heart of Peach Gardens, North Star Bakery grew out of a simple passion: returning bread to its essential roots through wild fermentation, regional grains, and time-honored baking methods.

The Story of Our Bakehouse

Head baker Owen Lindqvist started North Star after years of apprenticing in rustic bakeries across Northern Europe. When he returned to the Midwest, he was struck by how much commercial supermarket bread relied on artificial dough conditioners, commercial yeast, and bleached flours that stripped away grain flavor. He started baking naturally fermented sourdough in a converted garage oven and selling loaves at the neighborhood farmers market.

Neighbors fell in love with the crisp, caramelized crust and deep tangy flavor of the loaves. By the autumn of 2020, Owen opened our brick-and-mortar storefront on Maple Street, installing a stone deck oven and an open viewing kitchen where visitors can watch dough being shaped each morning.

Our Grain & Sourcing Philosophy

Great bread cannot exist without great farming. We believe a neighborhood bakery should strengthen the local food system, which is why we source our ingredients with care:

  • 100% Midwestern Grains: We buy whole wheat, rye, and heritage grains directly from family-run farms.
  • Fresh Stone-Milling: We grind a portion of our heritage whole grains on an in-house stone mill each morning to preserve nutrient-rich germ and aromatic grain oils.
  • Cultured Dairy & Seasonal Produce: Our butter, cream, and eggs come from local pastured dairy co-ops, and our fruit danish fillings change with local harvests.
  • Zero Artificial Additives: We never use chemical dough softeners, preservatives, or artificial dyes. Our sourdough starter has been fed daily with only flour and water since 2016.

Meet Our Bakehouse Crew

Our small team works through the night and into the early morning hours so our neighborhood has fresh bread at dawn:

Owen Lindqvist - Founder & Head Baker

Owen oversees sourdough culture maintenance, grain sourcing, and oven hearth management. When he isn't peeling loaves from the deck oven, he can be found testing heritage grain varieties with local farmers.

Amara Chen - Head Pastry Chef

Trained in classical French pastry arts, Amara leads our viennoiserie and cake program. She is the creative force behind our cardamom morning knots, seasonal laminated fruit danishes, and custom celebration cakes.
