Welcome to North Star Bakery

Welcome to our neighborhood bakery! Every morning before sunrise, we fire up our stone hearth deck ovens to bake naturally leavened sourdough breads, flaky butter croissants, and seasonal pastries for our community.

What We're Known For

We believe good bread takes time, local grains, and honest baking techniques. Here is what makes our bakery special to our neighbors and morning commuters:

  • 36-Hour Cold Fermentation: Our sourdough loaves ferment slowly for easy digestion and rich flavor.
  • Local Flour & Organic Grains: We partner with regional stone mills to source unbleached, non-GMO wheat and rye flours.
  • European-Style Lamination: Our croissants and morning buns are laminated by hand with high-fat farm butter.
  • Community Pre-Orders: We welcome pre-orders for weekend brunches, family dinners, and local gatherings.
